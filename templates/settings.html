Risk Management

Fixed = flat $ per trade. Percent = % of the balance recorded when the 15m window opened, so every trade in a window is the same size.

Per-Window Budget

30 with a 10% stop-loss = three stop-losses, then no more entries this window.

30 with a 30% take-profit = one winner, then stop for the window.

Both caps are measured against the risk-per-trade of the window they apply to, and reset the moment the next 15m window opens.

Entry Rule

Spot above the window's open → buy UP. Below → buy DOWN. When price is on the other side of the open, the held side is sold and reversed. There is no model and no probability gate — only the settings below shape it.

Price must be more than this far from the open to count as above/below. Inside the band there is no signal and an open position is simply held. 0 = flip on any distance — measured live, that reversed the position twice in one second on $3 of BTC movement, costing ~7% of stake each time.

Stop OPENING this many seconds before the 15m window ends (e.g. 20). A Fill-Or-Kill into a closing book is an unreliable fill, and a take-profit needs room to happen. Exits and stop-losses still run.

Skip the trade if the ask side can't absorb the whole stake. The stake is never trimmed to fit a thin book.

Measured on market movement — how far the position's value moved after the spread you paid to enter, not including it. (Measuring from the stake meant a ~50¢ position with a 5¢ spread was already ~10% down on fill, so the stop fired within seconds of entry, once even in the same second.) The spread is still paid, so a booked win/loss is about one spread smaller/larger than the % here. A take-profit ends the window; a stop-loss spends part of the loss budget and blocks that direction until price is on the other side of the open.
